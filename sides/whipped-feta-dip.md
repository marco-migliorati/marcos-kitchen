# Whipped Feta with Pistachios

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Last Tested:** 2026-09-30  
**Category:** Sides

A thick, creamy whipped feta with a texture closer to cream cheese than a loose dip. Finished simply with good olive oil and crushed pistachios.

## Ingredients

- 200 g feta, preferably a block feta in brine
- 100 g full-fat cream cheese, softened
- 15 g extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for finishing
- 10-15 g fresh lemon juice
- 1 small garlic clove, finely grated - optional
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 25-30 g roasted pistachios, roughly crushed

## Method

1. Remove the feta from the refrigerator 15-20 minutes before mixing. Drain well, pat dry, and crumble finely.

2. Add the softened cream cheese to the bowl of a KitchenAid mixer fitted with the paddle attachment.

3. Beat the cream cheese on medium-high speed for about 1 minute, until completely smooth.

4. Add the crumbled feta a little at a time, beating well between additions.

5. Once all of the feta has been incorporated, beat on medium-high speed for 2-3 minutes, stopping occasionally to scrape down the bowl. Continue until the mixture is smooth and creamy.

6. With the mixer running, slowly drizzle in the 15 g olive oil.

7. Add 10 g lemon juice, black pepper, and the optional grated garlic. Beat for another minute.

8. Taste and add up to another 5 g lemon juice if additional acidity is needed.

9. Refrigerate for 30-60 minutes to allow the whipped feta to firm up.

## Serving

Spread the whipped feta onto a shallow plate, using the back of a spoon to create swoops in the surface.

Drizzle generously with extra-virgin olive oil and scatter the crushed pistachios over the top.

Serve chilled or slightly below room temperature.

## Notes

- The 2:1 ratio of feta to cream cheese produces the thick, almost cream-cheese-like consistency intended for this recipe.
- Greek yogurt is intentionally omitted because it creates a lighter, looser dip.
- Softening the cream cheese and allowing the feta to warm slightly before mixing helps produce a smoother texture.
- The KitchenAid paddle attachment works particularly well for maintaining the dense texture of the finished spread.
