# Cuban Yellow Rice

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** About 6 servings  
**Category:** Sides & Salads

## Ingredients

- 2 cups long-grain white rice
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 small yellow onion, finely diced
- 1/2 green bell pepper, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, finely grated or minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 packet sazón with achiote, or about 1 1/2 tsp
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 3 1/2 cups chicken stock
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 1 bay leaf
- 1/2 cup frozen peas, optional
- 1/4 cup diced roasted red pepper or pimento, optional

## Method

1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then drain well.
2. Heat the olive oil in a heavy pot over medium heat.
3. Add the onion and green bell pepper and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
4. Add the garlic, cumin, oregano, sazón, and turmeric. Cook for about 30 seconds, until fragrant.
5. Add the drained rice and stir for 1-2 minutes to coat the grains in the seasoned oil.
6. Add the chicken stock, salt, and bay leaf.
7. Bring to a boil.
8. Reduce to low heat, cover tightly, and cook for about 18 minutes without lifting the lid.
9. Remove from the heat. If using peas and roasted red pepper or pimento, scatter them over the rice.
10. Cover again and let rest for 10 minutes.
11. Remove the bay leaf and fluff gently with a fork.
12. Taste and adjust salt before serving.

## Notes

- Achiote provides the characteristic yellow-orange color and savory flavor. Turmeric reinforces the color without needing a large quantity.
- Do not stir the rice once it begins simmering.
- The covered 10-minute rest is part of the cooking process and helps the grains finish evenly.
- Peas and pimento are optional depending on what the rice is being served with.
