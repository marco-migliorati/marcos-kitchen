# Brazilian Vinagrete

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star

## Ingredients
- 1 small onion or shallot
- 2 tomatoes
- 2 ribs of bell pepper
- Juice of ½ lime
- ½ cup vinegar
- Parsley or cilantro, to taste
- Good-quality extra-virgin olive oil, to taste — **added only when serving**
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Finely dice the onion or shallot, tomatoes, and bell pepper. Chop herbs.
2. Add vegetables and herbs to a bowl. Add lime juice, vinegar, salt, and pepper, and mix.
3. Cover and **refrigerate overnight** to let the flavors develop. (The original note specified at least 1 hour.)
4. **Just before serving**, transfer only the portion you plan to eat to another bowl.
5. Add extra-virgin olive oil **to the serving portion**, toss, and adjust seasoning.
6. Keep the remaining vinagrete **without oil** in the refrigerator.

## Kitchen note
The original note's ingredient quantities are retained. The overnight rest and portion-by-portion olive oil addition reflect the preferred house method.
