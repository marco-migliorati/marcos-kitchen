# Babaganouj

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Last Tested:** 2026-09-20  
**Yield:** About 2 cups  
**Category:** Sides & Salads

## Ingredients

- 2 large eggplants
- 60 g tahini
- 30-40 g fresh lemon juice
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- 1/2-3/4 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- Extra-virgin olive oil, for finishing

### Optional Finishing

- Chopped parsley
- Sumac or Aleppo pepper

## Method

### Fire-Roast the Eggplant

1. Leave the eggplants whole and unpeeled. Prick the skins a few times.
2. Heat the Ooni pizza oven.
3. Cook the eggplants in the oven, rotating periodically, until the skins are blackened and blistered on all sides and the eggplants are completely collapsed and very soft inside.
4. Remove from the oven and let cool until comfortable to handle.

### Prepare the Flesh

5. Split the eggplants lengthwise and scoop out the flesh.
6. Leave the seeds unless there are obvious pockets of large, mature brown seeds.
7. Place the flesh in a colander and drain for 15-30 minutes.
8. Chop or mash the drained eggplant by hand, leaving some texture rather than puréeing it completely smooth.

### Finish

9. Mix in the tahini, 30 g lemon juice, grated garlic, and 1/2 tsp kosher salt.
10. Taste and adjust with additional lemon juice, up to about 40 g total, and additional salt as needed.
11. Spoon into a shallow serving bowl.
12. Finish generously with extra-virgin olive oil and optional parsley, sumac, or Aleppo pepper.

## Equipment

- Ooni pizza oven
- Tongs
- Colander
- Mixing bowl
- Knife or fork for chopping/mashing

## Notes

- The tested version uses the Ooni pizza oven rather than a smoker.
- The goal is both intense exterior char and completely softened eggplant flesh.
- Draining the cooked eggplant concentrates the flavor and prevents watery babaganouj.
- Keep the texture rustic rather than blending completely smooth.
- Olive oil is primarily a finishing ingredient.
- A smoker variation can be added after it has been tested.

## Results

- ⭐ Gold Star after the 2026-09-20 Ooni cook.
- The high-heat fire-roasted version was delicious and produced the charred flavor desired for the dish.
