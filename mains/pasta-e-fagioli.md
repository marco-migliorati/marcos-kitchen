# Pasta e Fagioli

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Style:** Meatless family adaptation

## Ingredients
- ½ onion
- 1 small carrot
- 1 celery rib
- 3 garlic cloves
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 can white/navy beans, or 1 cup dried beans
- ½ cup tomato sauce
- **1 tbsp tomato paste, total**
- 2 large kale leaves
- ½ box ditalini pasta
- Salt, pepper, and plenty of Parmesan to serve

## Method
1. If using dried beans, cook separately until tender; the original note calls for pressure cooking with two parts water to one part beans for approximately 45 minutes. Verify doneness before proceeding.
2. Remove kale stems, roll the leaves, slice thinly, and set aside.
3. Dice onion, carrot, and celery. Sauté in 2 tbsp olive oil until soft and lightly browned, approximately 10 minutes.
4. Add the **single tablespoon** of tomato paste; stir and deglaze with a splash of water as needed.
5. Make a well, add remaining olive oil and garlic, and sauté about 2 minutes.
6. **Blend the sautéed soffritto** with an immersion blender to make a thick paste. This is key to the soup's hearty texture.
7. Add tomato sauce and cooked beans with their cooking liquid. If using canned beans, rinse and add about 1 cup water.
8. Add kale, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer gently for about 1 hour; add water as necessary.
9. **Cook ditalini separately** until al dente. Reserve some pasta water to adjust soup consistency.
10. Combine pasta and soup for serving. Finish with plenty of Parmesan.

## Family story
First tasted at zia Lina's apartment in Monte Mario, Rome. Her version included guanciale. This meatless adaptation was reconstructed from memory for a daughter while she was in college. Omit Parmesan for a vegan serving.
