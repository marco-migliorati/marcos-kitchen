# Chicken Piccata

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** Approximately 4 servings

## Ingredients
- 4 chicken breasts, sliced into thin cutlets
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- Flour, for light dredging
- 2 tbsp olive oil, plus 1 tbsp for aromatics if needed
- 1 lemon
- 1 shallot, chopped
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- ½ cup chicken broth
- ½ cup cooking wine (type not specified in original note)
- 3 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp capers, **rinsed**
- Chopped parsley

## Method
1. Season cutlets with salt, pepper, and cayenne. Rest 15 minutes.
2. Halve the lemon lengthwise. Juice one half; cut the other into thin slices.
3. Lightly dredge chicken in flour, shaking off excess.
4. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook cutlets in two batches, about 2–3 minutes per side, until browned. Set aside.
5. Add a little more oil if needed and sauté the shallot for 1 minute; add garlic briefly.
6. Add broth, wine, lemon juice, and lemon slices; simmer.
7. Return chicken to sauce and cook about 4 minutes over medium-low, until cooked through (**165°F / 74°C**).
8. Remove chicken. Turn off heat and whisk butter into the sauce. Stir in **rinsed** capers and parsley; spoon over cutlets.

## Kitchen lesson
Rinse the capers! A previous batch was unpleasantly salty and bitter when this step was missed.
