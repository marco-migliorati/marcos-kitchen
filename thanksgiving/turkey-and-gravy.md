# Thanksgiving Turkey & Gravy

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Signature technique:** The **aluminum-foil breast shield** is key.

## Dry brine (begin 3 days ahead)
- 4 oz salt
- 2 oz sugar

## Turkey-day browning mixture
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 4 tsp olive oil, divided (2 tsp in paste, 2 tsp in roasting pan)
- Whole turkey — **weight not specified in original note**
- Onion or other aromatics for roasting pan

## Gravy
- 1 onion, minced
- 1 small carrot, thinly sliced
- 5 parsley sprigs
- 2 bay leaves
- 5 tbsp flour
- 3¼ cups water
- ¼ cup white wine
- Reserved turkey drippings
- Turkey neck and giblets

## Turkey method
1. **Three days ahead:** Lift skin over breasts and thighs, rub salt-and-sugar brine underneath, tuck wings, tie legs, and refrigerate.
2. **Turkey day:** Heat roasting pan over a pizza stone or steel in a **500°F oven for 30 minutes**.
3. Pat turkey dry. Make a paste with baking powder and **2 tsp oil**; brush over skin.
4. **Form a triangular aluminum-foil shield over the breast.** Keep it in place during the initial high-heat roast; it protects white meat while the legs and thighs get a head start.
5. Carefully remove hot roasting pan, add remaining **2 tsp oil** and aromatics, and place turkey in pan with breast shielded.
6. Reduce oven to **425°F** and roast **45 minutes**.
7. Reduce to **325°F** and continue roasting, checking temperatures; original note estimates another **90 minutes**, but actual time depends on turkey size. Target **165°F breast** and **175°F thigh**.
8. Rest **45–60 minutes** before carving.

## Gravy method
1. Strain pan drippings into a fat separator, scraping up browned bits; let stand 10 minutes.
2. Separate fat and juices. The original note calls for **3 tbsp drippings** in a saucepan; use separated fat for browning and roux, retaining the juices for gravy.
3. Brown neck and giblets in saucepan; remove.
4. Sauté onion, carrot, parsley, and bay leaves about 7 minutes.
5. Stir in flour to form a roux. Gradually whisk in reserved pan juices.
6. Add water and wine, return neck and giblets, and simmer approximately 10 minutes.
7. Taste, season, strain, and serve.

## Notes
The foil **shield** is the distinguishing family technique. The original note does not record the bird's weight, so roasting times are approximate; rely on a thermometer. Handle the preheated roasting pan with extreme care.
