# Mashed Red Norland Potatoes

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Last Tested:** 2026-08-28  
**Yield:** About 4-6 servings  
**Category:** Sides & Salads

## Ingredients

- 2 lb (about 900 g) Red Norland potatoes
- 3-4 garlic cloves, peeled
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 71 g (5 Tbsp) butter
- 80 g warm milk
- 60 g sour cream
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Peel approximately 50-70% of the skin from the potatoes, leaving some red skin for texture and color.
2. Cut the potatoes into roughly 1 1/2-inch chunks.
3. Add the potatoes, garlic, water, and 1 tsp kosher salt to the Instant Pot.
4. Cook on **High Pressure for 6 minutes**.
5. Quick release the pressure.
6. Drain the potatoes thoroughly.
7. Return the potatoes and garlic to the warm pot and leave uncovered for **2-3 minutes** to steam-dry.
8. Add the butter first and mash by hand until incorporated.
9. Add the warm milk and sour cream.
10. Continue mashing gently until creamy.
11. Taste and adjust with additional salt and freshly ground black pepper.

## Equipment

- Instant Pot
- Colander
- Potato masher
- Digital scale

## Notes

- Red Norland potatoes are intentionally only partially peeled. Leaving some skin adds color and texture without overwhelming the mash.
- Steam-drying after pressure cooking helps prevent watery potatoes.
- Add the butter before the milk and sour cream.
- Mash by hand and avoid overworking the potatoes.
- Warm the milk before adding it.
