# Chicken Tinga

**Status:** 🧪 Testing  
**Category:** Mains  
**Yield:** About 8 crunchwraps, using roughly ½ cup of filling each.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb cooked smoked chicken, shredded
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 small onion, thinly sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 small can chipotles in adobo, **all the peppers and sauce**
- ¼ cup chicken broth to start, plus more as needed
- ½ teaspoon dried oregano
- ½ lime
- Salt to taste

## Directions

1. Finely mince the chipotles and reserve all the adobo sauce from the can.
2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion for **5 to 7 minutes**, until soft. Add the garlic and stir for 30 seconds.
3. Stir in the chipotles, adobo, oregano, and **¼ cup broth**. Bring to a gentle simmer.
4. Fold in the smoked chicken and heat gently, stirring, until it reaches **165°F**. Add more broth **1 tablespoon at a time** until the chicken is moist and coated. If liquid pools in the skillet, simmer uncovered briefly before assembling. [USDA safe reheating methods](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/What-methods-of-reheating-food-are-safe)
5. Finish with a squeeze of lime and taste before salting. For each crunchwrap, layer **½ cup filling, shredded Monterey Jack, a tostada, lime sour cream, and shredded lettuce**.

## Notes

The **¼ cup of broth** is a starting amount. Adjust it to how dry the shredded chicken is.
