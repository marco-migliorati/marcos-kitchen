# Cold-Pan Salmon

**Status:** ✅ Keeper  
**Inspiration:** America's Test Kitchen cold-pan approach, as recalled in kitchen notes

## Ingredients
- Skin-on salmon fillets
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Water and salt for a brief brine
- Lemon

## Method
1. Brine salmon in salted water for **15 minutes**. The original note does not specify the brine concentration.
2. Pat fillets thoroughly dry.
3. Season a **cold nonstick skillet** with salt and pepper; lay salmon in the pan skin-side down. **Do not add fat.**
4. Turn heat to medium-high. The original note calls for approximately **6–8 minutes per side**, but actual time depends on fillet thickness; monitor carefully and cook to your preferred safe doneness.
5. Finish the pan with lemon juice.

## Technique note
The distinguishing feature is starting in a cold, dry nonstick pan. The source note does not specify a gradual increase in heat beyond turning the burner to medium-high.
