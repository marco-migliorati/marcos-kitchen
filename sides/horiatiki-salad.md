# Horiatiki Salad

**Status:** 🧪 Testing  
**Last Tested:** 2026-09-20  
**Yield:** 4-6 servings  
**Category:** Sides & Salads

## Ingredients

### Pickled Shallot

- 1 shallot, thinly sliced
- 60 g (1/4 cup) red wine vinegar
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt
- Small pinch sugar, optional

### Salad

- 4 ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges or large chunks
- 1 English cucumber, cut into thick pieces
- Kosher salt, for salting the cucumber and seasoning the salad
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced
- Kalamata olives, to taste
- 1 block feta cheese
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Dried Greek oregano
- Freshly ground black pepper, optional
- Red wine vinegar, as needed

## Method

### Pickle the Shallot

1. Combine the sliced shallot, 60 g red wine vinegar, 1/4 tsp kosher salt, and optional pinch of sugar.
2. Refrigerate for several hours or up to the full day.
3. Drain before adding to the salad. Reserve the pickling liquid.

### Salt the Cucumber

4. Cut the cucumber into thick pieces and season lightly with kosher salt.
5. Let stand for 10-15 minutes.
6. Drain off the released liquid. Do not pickle the cucumber.

### Assemble

7. Combine the tomatoes, drained cucumber, green pepper, drained pickled shallot, and Kalamata olives.
8. Dress generously with extra-virgin olive oil.
9. Add a splash of the reserved shallot pickling liquid, or additional red wine vinegar, to taste.
10. Toss gently and adjust the salt and acidity.
11. Place the feta on top in a slab rather than crumbling it through the salad.
12. Finish with dried Greek oregano, another drizzle of olive oil, and black pepper if desired.

## Notes

- Pickled shallot replaces raw red onion for a more subtle onion flavor.
- The shallot can remain in its pickling liquid in the refrigerator throughout the day.
- Salt the cucumber separately for 10-15 minutes, then drain it before assembling the salad.
- Do not add the cucumber to the shallot pickle. Keeping it out of the acid preserves its fresh crunch.
