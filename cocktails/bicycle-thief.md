# Bicycle Thief

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** 1 cocktail  
**Category:** Cocktails

## Ingredients

- 1 oz gin
- 1 oz Campari
- 1 1/2 oz fresh grapefruit juice
- 1/2 oz fresh lemon juice
- 1/2 oz simple syrup (1:1)
- Club soda, to top
- Grapefruit or orange slice, for garnish

## Method

1. Add the gin, Campari, grapefruit juice, lemon juice, and simple syrup to a shaker with ice.
2. Shake until thoroughly chilled.
3. Strain into a Collins or highball glass filled with fresh ice.
4. Top with club soda.
5. Garnish with a grapefruit or orange slice.

## Notes

- Fresh grapefruit juice is central to the drink.
- The combination of grapefruit and Campari gives the cocktail a refreshing bittersweet character.
- Club soda lengthens and lightens the drink without adding additional sweetness.
- A 1:1 simple syrup keeps the sweetness restrained.
- The Bicycle Thief was created by Zachary Gelnaw-Rubin and Abraham Hawkins at Dutch Kills in New York City.
