# Buttermilk Sourdough Discard Pancakes

Choose the original overnight version or the same-day version when breakfast cannot wait. Both include optional vanilla and make about 24 medium pancakes.

Use sourdough discard at 100% hydration (equal weights flour and water). The two options use different ingredient quantities; follow one complete option.

## Option 1: Overnight

Mix the sponge the night before; finish the batter just before cooking. This version develops more fermented flavor.

### Ingredients

#### Overnight sponge

- 227 g sourdough discard (100% hydration, equal weights flour and water)
- 454 g buttermilk
- 240 g all-purpose flour
- 28 g granulated sugar

#### Final batter

- All of the overnight sponge
- 2 large eggs
- 57 g unsalted butter, melted and cooled until lukewarm
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional; our addition)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 3/4 teaspoon table salt

### Instructions

#### The night before

1. Stir the sponge ingredients together in a large bowl with room for expansion.
2. Cover and ferment overnight. King Arthur specifies approximately 12 hours at 65–70°F.

#### Before breakfast

1. Preheat a lightly greased griddle to approximately 350°F, or a skillet over medium heat.
2. Beat the eggs, melted butter, and optional vanilla together. Gently stir into the sponge.
3. Mix in the baking soda and salt evenly. The batter will become bubbly; begin cooking promptly.
4. Portion approximately 1/4 cup per pancake. Flip when bubbles open and the edges begin to set. Cook the second side until golden and the center is cooked through.

## Option 2: Same-day

No overnight preparation needed. Baking powder and baking soda provide the lift, with a milder fermented flavor than the overnight version.

### Ingredients

- 240 g all-purpose flour
- 50 g granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon table salt
- 454 g sourdough discard, stirred
- 360 g buttermilk, plus extra if needed
- 2 large eggs
- 56 g unsalted butter, melted and cooled until lukewarm
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract (optional)

### Instructions

1. Preheat a lightly greased griddle to approximately 350°F, or a skillet over medium heat.
2. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a large bowl.
3. In another bowl, whisk the discard, buttermilk, eggs, melted butter, and optional vanilla together. Cold discard from the refrigerator is fine.
4. Gently fold the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients until just combined. Small lumps are fine.
5. Rest for 5–10 minutes. The batter should be thick but pourable. If needed, gently fold in extra buttermilk 1 tablespoon at a time.
6. Portion approximately 1/4 cup per pancake. Flip when bubbles open and the edges begin to set. Cook the second side until golden and the center is cooked through.

## Cooking notes for either option

- Allow approximately 2–3 minutes on the first side and 1–2 minutes on the second. Adjust the heat as needed.
- Mix gently and do not press pancakes after flipping.
- Hold finished pancakes in a 200°F oven while cooking the remaining batter.
- For the overnight option, if breakfast is delayed after the rise, cover and refrigerate the sponge. Add the final batter ingredients when ready to cook.
- For the same-day option, cook after the short rest rather than holding the mixed batter for hours.

## Storage

Refrigerate leftover cooked pancakes for 1–2 days, or freeze for longer storage.

## Source

The overnight option is adapted from [King Arthur Baking: Classic Sourdough Pancakes](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/classic-sourdough-pancakes-recipe). Vanilla and the griddle temperature, approximate cooking times, and warm-holding instructions are additions to the source recipe.

The same-day option is our separate adaptation from this conversation, scaled to the double batch requested for a crowd. It is not King Arthur's published formula.
