# Mexican Vinegar Lettuce

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** About 4-6 servings  
**Category:** Sides & Salads

## Ingredients

- 1/2 large head iceberg lettuce, very thinly sliced
- 60 g (1/4 cup) white vinegar
- 30 g (2 Tbsp) water
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp sugar, optional

## Method

1. Slice the iceberg lettuce as thinly as possible.
2. Rinse if needed, then dry the lettuce thoroughly.
3. Combine the white vinegar, water, salt, and optional sugar. Stir until dissolved.
4. Place the lettuce in a large bowl.
5. Shortly before serving, drizzle the vinegar mixture over the lettuce and toss thoroughly.
6. Let stand for about 5-10 minutes, just long enough for the lettuce to lightly wilt and absorb the seasoning.
7. Toss again and serve.

## Notes

- The target is the thin, lightly vinegared iceberg lettuce commonly used as a garnish or filling for Mexican burritos, tortas, tacos, and similar dishes.
- Slice the lettuce very finely; the texture is an important part of the finished result.
- Dry the lettuce well so excess water does not dilute the vinegar.
- The lettuce should soften slightly while retaining some crunch.
- Do not dress too far in advance or the lettuce will lose too much texture.
- Adjust the vinegar and salt to taste depending on the amount of lettuce.

## Serving

- Burritos
- Tacos
- Tortas
- Beans and rice
- Grilled meats
