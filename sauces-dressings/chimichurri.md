# Chimichurri

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Last Tested:** 2026-03-07  
**Yield:** About 1 cup  
**Category:** Sauces & Condiments

## Ingredients

- 30 g fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, finely grated or minced
- 2 Tbsp fresh oregano leaves, finely chopped, or 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes, or to taste
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 60 g (1/4 cup) red wine vinegar
- 120 g (1/2 cup) extra-virgin olive oil

## Method

1. Finely chop the parsley and fresh oregano, if using.
2. Combine the parsley, oregano, garlic, red pepper flakes, salt, black pepper, and red wine vinegar.
3. Let the mixture sit for about 10 minutes so the vinegar can season the aromatics.
4. Stir in the olive oil.
5. Taste and adjust the salt, vinegar, and chile as needed.
6. Let the chimichurri rest for at least 30 minutes before serving.

## Make Ahead

- Chimichurri can be made the day before.
- Refrigerate covered.
- Bring it toward room temperature and stir well before serving.

## Using with Steak

- For skirt steak, use the chimichurri primarily as a finishing sauce rather than a long marinade.
- Season the steak simply with coarse salt before cooking.
- Cook over very high heat, including on a preheated pizza steel or flat-top if desired.
- Rest the steak, slice across the grain, and spoon chimichurri over the sliced meat.
- If using chimichurri before cooking, reserve a clean portion separately for serving.

## Notes

- Keep the sauce loose and spoonable rather than emulsifying or blending it smooth.
- Hand chopping preserves the texture of the herbs.
- Red wine vinegar provides the primary acidity.
- The sauce improves after a short rest as the garlic, herbs, vinegar, and oil come together.

## Results

- ⭐ Gold Star.
- Developed for grilled skirt steak and retained as the canonical Marco's Kitchen chimichurri.
