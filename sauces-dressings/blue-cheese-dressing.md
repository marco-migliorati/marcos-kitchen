# Blue Cheese Dressing

**Status:** ✅ Keeper

## Ingredients
- 4 tbsp buttermilk
- 2 tbsp sour cream
- ¼ cup mayonnaise
- 2 tsp white wine vinegar
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- Block blue cheese, crumbled — **amount to taste** (not specified in original note)
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method
1. Whisk buttermilk, sour cream, mayonnaise, vinegar, and grated garlic together.
2. Fold in crumbled block blue cheese.
3. Season to taste and refrigerate until serving.
