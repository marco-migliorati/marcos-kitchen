# Tavuk Şiş

**Status:** 🧪 Testing  
**Last Tested:** 2026-09-20  
**Yield:** About 4-6 servings  
**Category:** Mains

## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 2 lb (about 900 g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- Fresh herbs, finely chopped, to taste

### Marinade

- 120 g (about 1/2 cup) plain Greek yogurt
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 Tbsp tomato paste
- 3 garlic cloves, finely grated or minced
- 1 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 3/4 tsp turmeric
- 3/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- Aleppo pepper or cayenne, to taste
- Small pinch cinnamon, optional

## Method

1. Cut the chicken thighs into evenly sized skewer pieces.
2. Combine the yogurt, lemon juice, olive oil, tomato paste, garlic, spices, salt, and fresh herbs.
3. Add the chicken and toss thoroughly to coat.
4. Cover and refrigerate to marinate.
5. Before skewering, remove excess marinade. Leave a light coating, but avoid large clumps of yogurt.
6. Thread onto skewers with a small gap between pieces rather than packing them tightly.
7. Grill over high direct heat, turning as needed, until deeply browned and charred and the chicken is cooked through.
8. Serve immediately.

## Cooking

- **Heat:** High, direct grill heat
- **Goal:** Deep char and crisp edges without drying out the thighs
- **Target Temperature:** At least 165°F internal temperature

## Equipment

- Grill
- Skewers
- Mixing bowl
- Instant-read thermometer

## Notes

- The original version used 1 cup Greek yogurt and 1/2 lemon. The preferred direction is less yogurt, more lemon, and a stronger spice profile.
- Removing excess yogurt marinade before grilling helps the chicken char instead of steam.
- Leave small gaps between chicken pieces to expose more surface area to the heat.
- Fresh herbs were a successful addition in the previous batch. Exact herb selection and quantity should be recorded after the 2026-09-20 cook.
- Tonight's spice quantities are the stronger working formula and should be adjusted after tasting if needed.
