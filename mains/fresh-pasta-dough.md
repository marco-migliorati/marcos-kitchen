# Fresh Pasta Dough

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star

## Ingredients
- **90 g tipo 00 flour per large egg**, plus a little more as needed
- Large eggs (one per 90 g flour)

## Method
1. Make a well in the flour on a clean work surface. Beat the eggs and pour into the center.
2. Gradually incorporate flour until a shaggy dough forms. Add only as much additional flour as needed.
3. Knead firmly for **10 minutes**: press with both hands, turn the dough 90 degrees, fold, and repeat.
4. Wrap tightly and rest at room temperature for **30 minutes**.
5. Roll with a pasta machine or rolling pin and cut to the desired shape.

## Notes
Start at 90 g flour per egg rather than 100 g; it is easier to add flour than to correct an overly dry dough.
