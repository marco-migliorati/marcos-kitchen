# Smoked Chivalini Sausage

Thin, coiled Italian pork sausage cooked gently in the smoker.

**Status:** 🧪 Testing  
**Category:** Mains  
**Equipment:** Smoker and meat thermometer

## Ingredients

- Fresh Chivalini pork sausage, coiled
- Smoking wood or pellets of your choice

## Method

1. Preheat the smoker to **225°F**.
2. Place the sausage flat on the grate. Gently loosen the coil slightly, if possible, to let smoke and heat circulate between the loops. Leave the casing intact.
3. Smoke for approximately **1–1½ hours**, starting temperature checks after **45 minutes**. Timing depends on thickness and how tightly the sausage is coiled.
4. Cook to **160°F internally**. Insert a thin thermometer probe sideways into the center of the sausage and check several spots, especially the inner loops.
5. Optional: Finish briefly on a hot grill or flattop for a more browned casing.
6. Rest for **5 minutes**, then cut and serve.

## Notes

- **Use internal temperature, rather than time, to determine when the sausage is done.**
- Keep the casing intact when adjusting the coil.
