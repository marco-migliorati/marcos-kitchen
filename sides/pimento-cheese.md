# Pimento Cheese

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** About 2 cups  
**Category:** Sauces & Condiments

## Ingredients

- 225 g (8 oz) sharp cheddar cheese, freshly grated
- 115 g (4 oz) cream cheese, softened
- 120 g (about 1/2 cup) mayonnaise
- 115 g (4 oz) diced pimentos, drained well
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper, optional
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Kosher salt, to taste

## Method

1. Grate the cheddar using the large holes of a box grater.
2. In a bowl, mix the softened cream cheese and mayonnaise until combined.
3. Stir in the garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, cayenne if using, and black pepper.
4. Fold in the grated cheddar and well-drained pimentos.
5. Mix until evenly combined while retaining the texture of the grated cheese.
6. Taste and add salt only if needed.
7. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving to let the flavors come together.
8. Let sit briefly at room temperature before serving if a softer, more spreadable texture is desired.

## Notes

- Grate the cheddar fresh rather than using pre-shredded cheese.
- Drain the pimentos thoroughly so the finished spread does not become watery.
- Sharp cheddar provides the primary flavor, so use a cheese you enjoy eating on its own.
- Keep some texture in the mixture rather than blending it completely smooth.
- Cayenne is optional and should provide background heat rather than dominate.

## Serving

- Crackers
- Toast or bread
- Sandwiches
- Burgers
- Celery or other crisp vegetables

## Results

- ⭐ Gold Star.
