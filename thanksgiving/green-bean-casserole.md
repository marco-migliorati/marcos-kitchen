# Green Bean Casserole (Babish-Inspired)

**Status:** ⭐ Family keeper  
**Source:** [Basics with Babish — Thanksgiving Sides](https://www.babi.sh/recipes/thanksgivingsides)

## Components
- Fresh green beans, trimmed
- Cremini mushrooms
- Butter and garlic
- Flour
- Cognac
- Chicken stock
- Heavy cream
- A dash of soy sauce
- **Store-bought crispy fried onions** for topping

## Method
1. Blanch green beans in salted boiling water until crisp-tender. Chill promptly and drain well.
2. Sauté mushrooms in butter until browned; add garlic.
3. Stir in flour to thicken, then add a splash of cognac and chicken stock, stirring smooth.
4. Add cream and soy sauce; simmer until thick enough to coat beans.
5. Combine green beans and sauce in a baking dish. Add some store-bought crispy onions.
6. Bake at **350°F for 15–20 minutes**; top with remaining crispy onions and bake **5–10 minutes** more.

## Thanksgiving-tested shortcut
Homemade fried onions were attempted one year. Frying them from scratch took too long, created a mess, and competed with too many other Thanksgiving tasks. Store-bought crispy onions are the preferred family method.
