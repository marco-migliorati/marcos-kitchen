# Turkey Meatloaf (ATK-Inspired)

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Source:** Adapted from an America's Test Kitchen turkey meatloaf video, transcribed into personal cooking notes.  
**Category:** Mains (not Thanksgiving)

## Ingredients
- Ground turkey — **quantity not recorded in original note** (confirm before cooking)
- 3 tbsp butter
- ½ onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- A pinch of baking soda (mentioned in method, not measured in source)
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 egg yolks
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 3 tbsp quick oats
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- Salt and pepper

### Glaze
- 1 cup ketchup
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- 2 tbsp cider vinegar
- ½ tsp Tabasco

## Method
1. Melt butter with a pinch of baking soda. Sauté onion about 5 minutes, adding garlic for the last minute.
2. Add Worcestershire and reduce about 1 minute. Cool.
3. Mix onion mixture with egg yolks, mustard, oats, cornstarch, and Parmesan.
4. Add ground turkey and mix gently by hand. Shape into a 9 × 5-inch loaf on a foil-lined rack over a sheet pan.
5. Simmer glaze ingredients for 5 minutes; brush over loaf.
6. Bake at **375°F for 40 minutes**. Brush with additional glaze and continue approximately **35–40 minutes**, adjusting to actual loaf size and temperature.
7. Cook ground turkey to **165°F (74°C)** internally; rest about 20 minutes before slicing.
