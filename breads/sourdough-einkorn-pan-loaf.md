# Sourdough Einkorn Pan Loaf

**Status:** 🧪 Testing  
**Last Tested:** Not yet tested - October 2026  
**Yield:** 1 loaf  
**Category:** Breads  
**Version:** Test 1 - 40% Einkorn

Adapted from the Gold Star Sourdough Pan Loaf to incorporate Jovial all-purpose einkorn flour.

## Levain

- 18 g active sourdough starter
- 18 g bread flour
- 18 g whole wheat flour
- 36 g water

**Total levain:** 90 g

Feed the starter. Approximately 4 hours later, begin the autolyse.

## Dough

- 185 g bread flour
- 40 g regular all-purpose flour
- 180 g Jovial all-purpose einkorn flour
- 45 g whole wheat flour
- 320 g water, approximately 95°F
- 9 g fine sea salt
- All 90 g of ripe levain

**Total dough weight:** Approximately 869 g.

## Method

### Autolyse

1. Combine all four flours with all 320 g of the 95°F water.
2. Mix until no dry flour remains.
3. Cover and rest for **30 minutes**.

### Mix

4. Add the ripe levain and mix gently until incorporated.
5. Cover and rest for 15 minutes.
6. Add the salt and work it into the dough.
7. Gently stretch and fold the dough to finish incorporating the salt.
8. Return the dough to its container.

Avoid the original 20 aggressive slap-and-fold movements because einkorn has a more delicate gluten structure.

### Folds and Bulk Fermentation

9. Perform four gentle folds:
   - Fold 1, then rest 30 minutes.
   - Fold 2, then rest 30 minutes.
   - Fold 3, then rest 30 minutes.
   - Fold 4.
10. After the fourth fold, cover and leave the dough undisturbed until approximately 30-50% larger.
11. Watch for a slightly domed surface and visible fermentation activity.

Do not rely exclusively on the original 2½-hour final bulk fermentation. Use dough expansion as the primary indicator.

### Shape and Pan

12. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured counter.
13. Gently shape into a loaf without tearing the dough.
14. Place seam-side down into a thoroughly greased loaf pan.
15. Cover and allow some visible expansion at room temperature without waiting for the dough to double.

**Room-temperature proof duration:** To be determined during testing.

### Cold Proof

16. Cover securely and refrigerate for 24 hours.
17. Bake directly from the refrigerator.

The original recipe allows 24-48 hours, but the first einkorn experiment will use 24 hours.

## Baking

### Florida - Experimental Convection Schedule

1. Preheat the oven to 450°F convection.
2. Bake covered for 30 minutes.
3. Remove the cover and reduce the temperature to 425°F.
4. Bake uncovered for 12 minutes.
5. Rotate the pan.
6. Bake for another 12 minutes, checking earlier if browning rapidly.
7. Target an internal temperature of approximately 205-210°F.
8. Remove from the pan and cool completely on a rack before slicing.

This schedule is based on the successful Maine convection method and requires Florida validation.

## Experimental Changes

| --- | --- | --- |

| Main dough water | 340 g | 320 g |
| Autolyse | 60 minutes | 30 minutes |
| Slap-and-fold | 20 repetitions | Omitted |
| Folds | 6 | 4 |
| Final bulk | 2½ hours | Rise-based |
| Cold proof | 24-48 hours | 24 hours |

## Notes

- Einkorn represents 40% of the main dough flour, or approximately 37% of total flour including the levain.
- The 40 g regular AP flour substitution was necessary because only 185 g bread flour was available.
- All 320 g water is incorporated during the autolyse. No water is reserved.
- Einkorn has weaker gluten characteristics than modern bread wheat. Handle gently and avoid excessive kneading.
- The loaf pan provides support for the softer dough.
- Maintain the original Gold Star Sourdough Pan Loaf as a separate recipe.
