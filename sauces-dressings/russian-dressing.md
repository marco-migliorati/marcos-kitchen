# Russian Dressing

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** About 1 cup  
**Category:** Sauces & Condiments

## Ingredients

- 120 g (1/2 cup) mayonnaise
- 60 g (1/4 cup) ketchup
- 15 g (1 Tbsp) prepared horseradish
- 10 g (2 tsp) Worcestershire sauce
- 5 g (1 tsp) Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp hot sauce
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp onion powder
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Kosher salt, only if needed

## Method

1. Combine the mayonnaise, ketchup, horseradish, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard, hot sauce, smoked paprika, onion powder, and garlic powder.
2. Stir until completely combined.
3. Add freshly ground black pepper.
4. Taste before adding salt. The Worcestershire sauce and other ingredients may provide enough seasoning.
5. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.
6. Stir again before using.

## Notes

- Prepared horseradish gives Russian dressing its characteristic sharper edge compared with Thousand Island.
- Adjust the horseradish and hot sauce to taste.
- Make ahead when possible so the flavors have time to meld.
- Keep refrigerated.

## Serving

- Reuben sandwiches
- Burgers and sandwiches
- Dipping sauce
- Salad dressing

## Results

- ⭐ Gold Star.
- Developed as the Marco's Kitchen dressing for Reuben sandwiches.
