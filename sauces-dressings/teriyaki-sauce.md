# Teriyaki Sauce

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** About 1 cup  
**Category:** Sauces & Condiments

## Ingredients

- 120 g (1/2 cup) soy sauce
- 120 g (1/2 cup) mirin
- 120 g (1/2 cup) sake
- 50 g sugar

## Method

1. Combine the soy sauce, mirin, sake, and sugar in a small saucepan.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
3. Reduce the heat and simmer gently until the sauce is slightly reduced and glossy, about 10-15 minutes.
4. Remove from the heat and let cool.
5. Use as a glaze, finishing sauce, or marinade as appropriate.

## Substitutions

### If Mirin Is Unavailable

For each 120 g (1/2 cup) mirin, substitute:

- 120 g dry sake or dry white wine
- 2 tsp sugar

### If Sake Is Unavailable

Substitute an equal amount of dry white wine or dry sherry.

### If Neither Mirin nor Sake Is Available

Use dry white wine for both and add enough additional sugar to replace the sweetness normally supplied by the mirin.

## Notes

- Traditional teriyaki is built around roughly equal parts soy sauce, mirin, and sake, with sugar adjusted to taste.
- The sauce thickens further as it cools.
- Avoid reducing it too aggressively; the goal is glossy and pourable rather than syrupy.
- For grilling, apply sugary teriyaki toward the end of cooking to reduce the chance of scorching.

## Results

- ⭐ Gold Star.
- Retained as the canonical Marco's Kitchen teriyaki sauce.
