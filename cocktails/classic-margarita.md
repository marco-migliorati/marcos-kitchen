# Classic Margarita

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Last Tested:** 2026  
**Yield:** 1 cocktail  
**Category:** Cocktails

## Ingredients

- 2 oz tequila
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 3/4 oz orange liqueur
- Agave syrup, to taste
- Salt for rim, optional
- Lime wedge or wheel, for garnish

## Method

1. Add tequila, lime juice, orange liqueur, and agave to a shaker with ice.
2. Shake well.
3. Strain over fresh ice or serve up.
4. Garnish with lime.
