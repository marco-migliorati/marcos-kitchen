# Sourdough Pan Loaf

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Last Tested:** 2026  
**Yield:** 2 loaves  
**Category:** Breads

## Levain

- 35 g active sourdough starter
- 35 g bread flour
- 35 g whole wheat flour
- 70 g water

Feed the starter. Approximately 4 hours later, begin the autolyse.

## Dough

- 810 g bread flour
- 90 g whole wheat flour
- 680 g water, approximately 95°F
- 18 g fine sea salt
- All of the levain

## Method

### Autolyse

1. Approximately 4 hours after feeding the starter, combine the bread flour, whole wheat flour, and all 680 g of the 95°F water.
2. Mix until no dry flour remains.
3. Cover and rest for 1 hour.

### Mix

4. Add the ripe levain and mix thoroughly until incorporated.
5. Cover and rest for 15 minutes.
6. Add the salt and work it into the dough.
7. Turn the dough onto the counter and slap it against the counter approximately 20 times.
8. Return the dough to its container.

### Folds and Bulk Fermentation

9. Perform 6 folds total:
   - Fold 1, then rest 15 minutes.
   - Fold 2, then rest 15 minutes.
   - Fold 3, then rest 15 minutes.
   - Fold 4, then rest 30 minutes.
   - Fold 5, then rest 30 minutes.
   - Fold 6.
10. After the sixth fold, cover the dough and leave it undisturbed on the counter for 2 1/2 hours.

### Divide and Pan

11. Divide the dough into 2 equal portions.
12. Shape each portion into a loaf.
13. Place the loaves into well-greased loaf pans.
14. Allow the shaped loaves to rest in the pans before refrigeration. **Room-temperature proof duration to be confirmed.**
15. Cover and refrigerate for 24-48 hours.

## Baking

### Maine - Convection Oven

1. Preheat the oven to 450°F convection.
2. Bake the loaves covered for 30 minutes.
3. Remove the covers and reduce the oven temperature to 425°F.
4. Bake uncovered for 12 minutes.
5. Rotate the pans.
6. Bake for another 12 minutes, or until deeply browned and fully baked.
7. Remove from the pans and cool completely on a rack before slicing.

## Equipment

- Large mixing bowl or dough tub
- Digital scale
- Dough scraper
- 2 loaf pans
- Covers suitable for the loaf pans
- Cooling rack

## Notes

- All 680 g of water goes into the autolyse. The source boule recipe reserves part of the water, but the all-at-once method is the tested preference for this pan loaf.
- Grease the loaf pans thoroughly.
- This pan loaf evolved from a boule-style sourdough formula but is maintained as its own recipe.

## Results

- Reliable everyday pan sourdough.
- Six folds build strength before the final undisturbed bulk fermentation.
- The 24-48 hour cold proof provides scheduling flexibility.
