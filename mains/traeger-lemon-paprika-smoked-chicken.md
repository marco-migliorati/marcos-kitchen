# Traeger Lemon-Paprika Smoked Chicken

**Status:** 🧪 Testing  
**Yield:** 2 whole chickens, approximately 5 lb each  
**Category:** Mains  
**Cook time:** About 2–3 hours, plus 15 minutes to rest  
**Pellets:** Apple for mild smoke

## Ingredients

- 2 whole chickens, about 5 lb each
- 25 g kosher salt, divided between the birds
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon sweet paprika
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons onion powder
- 2 teaspoons black pepper
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 lemon, zested and cut into wedges for serving

## Method

1. Remove the giblets and pat the chickens dry. Divide the salt evenly between them, seasoning the skin and a little under the breast skin. If time allows, refrigerate uncovered for **4–24 hours**. Use less salt if the chickens are packed in an added salt solution.
2. Preheat the Traeger to **225°F** with the lid closed. Mix the olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, pepper, thyme, and lemon zest. Rub the mixture evenly over both birds.
3. Set the chickens directly on the grate, breast-side up, with a few inches between them. Insert the wired probe from the side into the center of the thickest part of one breast, keeping the tip clear of bone and the cavity. Smoke for **about 1 hour**.
4. Raise the Traeger to **350°F** to brown the skin. Start checking after about **45 minutes** at the higher setting, or sooner if the probe reaches **165°F**. Check both birds separately. Pull each chicken once the thickest part of the breast and the innermost parts of the thigh and wing all reach **165°F**, with the thermometer clear of bone. If one bird finishes first, pull it while the other continues cooking.
5. Rest for **15 minutes**, then carve and squeeze fresh lemon over the meat.

## Notes

- Use internal temperature rather than elapsed time to determine doneness.
- Check each bird independently because they may finish at different times.
