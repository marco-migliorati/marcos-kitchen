# Marco's Caipirinha

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** 1 cocktail  
**Style:** Shaken, with simple syrup

## Ingredients
- 2 oz (60 ml) cachaça
- 1 medium lime
- ¾ oz (22 ml) simple syrup (1:1 sugar and water) — **reconstructed starting measurement**
- Ice

## Method
1. Wash lime. Cut **lengthwise into quarters** and remove the thick white **central rib** from each quarter.
2. Cut into smaller wedges and add to a cocktail shaker.
3. Muddle **gently** to release juice without shredding the peel.
4. Add cachaça and simple syrup, then ice.
5. Shake vigorously for **10–15 seconds**.
6. **Pour the entire contents**, including the muddled lime and the **same shaker ice**, into a rocks glass. Do not strain or replace the ice.

## Notes
Removing the white central rib reduces bitterness. Simple syrup incorporates more evenly than granulated sugar. This is a long-standing family favorite; the drink itself is Gold Star, although the exact syrup-to-cachaça ratio was reconstructed from memory and can be adjusted to taste.
