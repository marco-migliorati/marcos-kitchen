# Creamy Fettuccine Alfredo

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Style:** American-style cream sauce  
**Inspiration:** Basics with Babish / America's Test Kitchen-style preparation as discussed during recipe review

## Ingredients
- 9 oz fresh fettuccine
- 1½ cups heavy cream, divided (1 cup + ½ cup cold)
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 oz (57 g) freshly grated Parmesan
- 1 tsp black pepper
- ⅛ tsp nutmeg
- Approximately ¼ cup reserved pasta water, as needed
- Salt for pasta water

## Method
1. Cook fresh fettuccine in salted water until just tender. Reserve pasta water.
2. In a wide saucepan, gently reduce **1 cup heavy cream** with butter until slightly thickened.
3. Stir in the remaining **½ cup cold cream**.
4. Add hot drained fettuccine and toss gently.
5. Off heat or over very low heat, add freshly grated Parmesan a little at a time, tossing to emulsify without clumping.
6. Season with pepper and nutmeg; add reserved pasta water in small splashes until silky and glossy.
7. Serve immediately.

## Notes
This is the preferred cream-based preparation, replacing the older Apple Notes egg-yolk version. Grate Parmesan fresh.
