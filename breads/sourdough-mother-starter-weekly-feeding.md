# Sourdough Mother Starter - Weekly Feeding

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star - Einkorn Blend  
**Last Tested:** October 2026 - first einkorn feeding planned  
**Yield:** 100 g starter (scalable)  
**Category:** Breads / Techniques  
**Version:** Test 1 - 50% Whole Wheat Einkorn

## Overview

Weekly maintenance feeding for an established sourdough mother starter. Keep the usual **1:2:2 starter:flour:water ratio**, with a trial blend of bread flour and Jovial whole wheat einkorn. Ferment in a temperature-controlled countertop appliance at **78°F**, then refrigerate until the next weekly feeding.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Mature sourdough mother starter | 20 g |
| Bread flour | 20 g |
| Jovial whole wheat einkorn flour | 20 g |
| Water | 40 g |
| **Total** | **100 g** |

**Scaling:** For every 1 part retained starter, add 1 part bread flour, 1 part whole wheat einkorn flour, and 2 parts water. This is a 1:2:2 feeding by total flour.

## Method

1. Retain **20 g** of the established mother starter in a clean container. Discard or use the excess starter separately.
2. Add **40 g water** and stir to disperse the starter.
3. Add **20 g bread flour** and **20 g Jovial whole wheat einkorn flour**. Mix thoroughly until no dry flour remains.
4. Mark the starter's initial height on the container and cover loosely or use the appliance's normal lid arrangement.
5. Place in the countertop fermentation appliance set to **78°F**.
6. **Begin checking after 3 hours.** The planned observation window is **3-4 hours**, but the starter's activity matters more than the timer. Look for a noticeable rise, abundant bubbles, and a rounded or just-leveling surface.
7. **Refrigerate while active and near peak, ideally before significant collapse.** The starter does not need to double exactly, especially with einkorn flour.
8. Store refrigerated until the next weekly feeding.

## If the Starter Peaks or Collapses Early

A starter that has risen and collapsed is usually overripe, not dead. It may become thin or watery as acidity and enzymes break down its structure. If it smells normally sour or yeasty and has no signs of contamination, refrigerate it or refresh it again if it is severely overripe and you want to rebuild activity before storage. A small amount of liquid separation is not, by itself, a reason to discard it.

**Discard the starter if you see fuzzy mold or unusual pink/orange discoloration.**

## Notes

- This is a **mother-starter maintenance feeding**, not the separate 90 g levain build used in the Sourdough Einkorn Pan Loaf.
- A bread-flour-maintained mother starter can still be used to make einkorn bread; switching the mother starter to einkorn is optional.
- Keep a small portion of the established starter on its original flour as a backup during the first trial if convenient.
- The 3-4-hour window is a **check-in time**, not a mandatory fermentation endpoint.
