# Water-First Sautéed Mushrooms

**Status:** ✅ Keeper  
**Yield:** Based on 3 lb mushrooms

## Ingredients
- 3 lb mushrooms, any variety, cut evenly
- ¼ cup water
- 3 garlic cloves
- ½ tsp coarse salt
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 2 tbsp butter
- ¼ red onion or 1 shallot, chopped
- Fresh thyme (preferred), or rosemary or parsley
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method
1. Add mushrooms and water to a large frying pan. Cook over high heat until the water evaporates and the mushrooms shrink considerably, approximately **15 minutes**.
2. Meanwhile, pound garlic and coarse salt in a mortar to a paste; stir in lemon juice.
3. Stir garlic-lemon paste into reduced mushrooms.
4. Make a well in the center. Add butter and chopped shallot or onion; sauté until browned and caramelized.
5. Combine with mushrooms, season, and finish with thyme or other fresh herbs.

## Technique
Cooking with water first helps the mushrooms release moisture before the butter is added for browning.
