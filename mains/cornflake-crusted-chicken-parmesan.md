# Cornflake-Crusted Chicken Parmesan

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** 18 thin cutlets  
**Category:** Mains

A crunchy chicken Parmesan made with a cornflake and breadcrumb crust. The chicken is fried in grapeseed oil, then lightly sauced and topped with mozzarella and Parmesan so the crust stays crisp.

## Chicken

- 6 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- Tenderloins removed and flattened
- Main breasts butterflied and flattened into 12 cutlets
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper

This produces 18 prepared cutlets.

Remove the chicken from the refrigerator about 15-20 minutes before breading. Pat dry and season both sides lightly with kosher salt and black pepper.

## Flour Dredge

- 150 g all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper

Combine in a wide, shallow dish.

## Egg Wash

- 5 large eggs
- 2 Tbsp water
- Pinch of kosher salt

Beat thoroughly in a second shallow dish.

## Cornflake Breading

- 180 g cornflakes
- 120 g breadcrumbs
- 60 g finely grated Parmesan
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper

Crush the cornflakes by hand or with a rolling pin. Aim for mostly breadcrumb-sized pieces while leaving some larger flakes for texture. Do not pulverize them into powder.

Combine the crushed cornflakes with the breadcrumbs, Parmesan, oregano, garlic powder, and black pepper.

Place about half of the breading mixture into the working dish and reserve the remainder. Replenish as needed so the entire mixture does not become wet from the egg wash.

## Bread the Chicken

Working one piece at a time:

1. Coat completely in the seasoned flour.
2. Shake off the excess flour.
3. Dip into the egg wash, coating both sides.
4. Allow excess egg to drip off.
5. Press firmly into the cornflake mixture, coating both sides.
6. Transfer to a wire rack or sheet pan.

Allow the breaded chicken to rest for 10-15 minutes before frying. This helps the coating adhere.

## Fry

- Grapeseed oil

Add approximately 1/4 inch of grapeseed oil to a large griddle or skillet.

For a Le Creuset griddle spanning the stove bridge, set the heat to approximately **6 out of 10**.

Once the oil is hot, fry the chicken in batches without crowding the cooking surface.

Cook approximately 2-3 minutes per side, flipping once, until the crust is deeply golden and crisp and the chicken reaches 165°F internally.

Transfer the cooked chicken to a wire rack.

### Between Batches

After each batch, remove loose cornflakes, breadcrumbs, Parmesan, and other debris from the oil before frying the next batch. This prevents accumulated breading from burning and affecting the flavor of subsequent batches.

Add additional grapeseed oil as necessary and allow it to come back to temperature before continuing.

## Assemble the Chicken Parmesan

- Prepared tomato sauce
- Mozzarella
- Finely grated Parmesan

Heat the broiler.

Arrange all 18 fried cutlets on sheet pans.

Spoon a restrained amount of tomato sauce over the center of each cutlet, leaving plenty of the crispy perimeter exposed.

Top with mozzarella and additional grated Parmesan.

Broil until the cheese is melted, bubbling, and beginning to brown.

Serve immediately, with additional warm tomato sauce on the side if desired.

## Notes

- Six chicken breasts produced plenty of chicken for this batch.
- 150 g flour is sufficient; the original 200 g batch left a significant amount unused.
- Five eggs are needed for 18 cutlets; four eggs were not sufficient.
- Grapeseed oil worked well for frying.
- A heat setting of 6/10 worked well on the Le Creuset griddle.
- Cleaning loose breading from the oil between batches prevents burnt bits from accumulating.
- Keep the tomato sauce concentrated toward the center of each cutlet to preserve the cornflake crust.

## The Goal

**Crispy chicken first, chicken Parmesan second.**

The cornflakes provide a crunchy, craggy exterior while the finer breadcrumbs fill the gaps and create a cohesive crust. A restrained amount of sauce keeps the chicken crisp through the final broil.

## Results

- ⭐ Gold Star.
- First full batch successfully tested with 18 cutlets from 6 chicken breasts.
- Final adjustments after the cook: reduce flour to 150 g, increase egg wash to 5 eggs, use grapeseed oil, and clean the griddle between batches.
