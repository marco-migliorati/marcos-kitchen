# Assiette Tunisienne Dressing

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star

## Ingredients
- ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp water
- 1 scant tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Method
1. Whisk vinegar, water, and mustard until smooth.
2. Slowly whisk in olive oil until blended.
3. Add red pepper flakes, salt, and black pepper to taste.
