# Negroni Sbagliato

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** 1 cocktail  
**Category:** Cocktails

## Ingredients

- 1 oz Campari
- 1 oz sweet red vermouth
- 1 oz Prosecco
- Orange slice or orange peel, for garnish

## Method

1. Fill a rocks glass with ice.
2. Add the Campari and sweet vermouth.
3. Stir briefly to chill and combine.
4. Add the Prosecco.
5. Give the drink one gentle stir.
6. Garnish with an orange slice or expressed orange peel.

## Notes

- **Sbagliato** means “mistaken” or “messed up” in Italian.
- The drink replaces the gin in a traditional Negroni with sparkling wine.
- Use sweet red vermouth, not dry vermouth.
- Keep the Prosecco cold and add it last to preserve the carbonation.
- The equal-parts ratio makes the recipe easy to scale.
- Serve over ice for a refreshing, lower-proof alternative to a traditional Negroni.
