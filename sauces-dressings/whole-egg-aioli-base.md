# Whole-Egg Aioli / Mayonnaise Base

**Status:** ✅ Keeper  
**Technique:** Immersion blender

## Ingredients
- 1 **whole egg**
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Salt and pepper
- **¾ cup (180 ml) oil total:** ⅜ cup (90 ml) neutral oil + ⅜ cup (90 ml) olive oil
- Optional: cayenne, red pepper flakes

### Garlic variation
- 1–2 garlic cloves, finely minced or mashed to a paste

### Cilantro variation (from original notes)
- ½ cup prepared base
- ½ cup packed cilantro
- 1–2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- ¼ tsp cumin
- Salt to taste

## Method
1. Add whole egg, Dijon, vinegar, lemon, and seasonings to a **tall, narrow container**.
2. **Add all the oil to the cup before blending**, using the measured 50/50 neutral and olive oil blend. Place the immersion-blender head firmly on the bottom.
3. Blend without lifting until a thick emulsion forms at the base; then **slowly raise** the blender to incorporate the oil already in the cup. **Do not drizzle the oil in while blending.**
4. **If the aioli is runny, add a little more oil and blend again** until it thickens.
5. Taste and adjust acidity and salt.
6. For garlic aioli, add garlic paste and blend briefly. For cilantro aioli, blend the listed variation ingredients into ½ cup finished base.

## Uses
Keep plain for pimento cheese or dressings; add garlic for aioli.

## Food safety
This contains raw egg. Use pasteurized eggs when serving people at higher risk of foodborne illness, and refrigerate promptly. Start with **¾ cup total oil**, half neutral and half olive. If the aioli is runny, add more oil and blend again. Add the entire initial ¾ cup **before** switching on the immersion blender.
