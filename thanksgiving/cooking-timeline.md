# Thanksgiving Cooking Timeline

**Status:** 🧪 Planning draft  
**Goal:** Coordinate the holiday dishes from their individual recipes without oven conflicts.

This schedule is reconstructed from the current recipes; the original consolidated timing note was not found. **Set a serving time and turkey weight before calculating clock times.**

## Three days before
- Begin the turkey dry brine.

## Day before
- Review recipes and shopping quantities.
- Cube and dry baguette for stuffing (or complete early on Thanksgiving).
- Prep vegetables for stuffing and green bean casserole.
- Check that store-bought crispy onions are on hand.

## Thanksgiving day — relative to serving
1. **Turkey:** Preheat roasting pan 30 minutes at 500°F; roast 45 minutes at 425°F, then approximately 90 minutes at 325°F, **until thermometer targets are reached**. The breast shield stays central to the technique.
2. **Turkey rest:** Allow 45–60 minutes. During the rest, make gravy from drippings.
3. **Oven transition:** After turkey comes out, raise oven to **350°F** for stuffing (30 minutes) and green bean casserole (15–20 minutes plus 5–10 minutes with final onions). Coordinate rack space and casserole size.
4. **Stovetop / Instant Pot:** Make Red Norland mashed potatoes (6 minutes at high pressure, plus pressure buildup, release, draining, mashing, and seasoning).
5. **Finish:** Keep hot dishes safely warm, carve turkey near serving, and serve gravy hot.

## Planning caveats
- The turkey weight, oven capacity, casserole sizes, and serving time are not specified.
- Treat recipe durations as **estimates**, not a validated clock-time schedule.
- Stuffing and green bean casserole may share a 350°F oven if space and heat circulation allow.
- Use the thermometer, not the timeline, to decide when the turkey is done.
