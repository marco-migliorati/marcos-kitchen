# Chana Masala with Instant Pot Onion Masala

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Source:** Adapted from Urvashi Pitre's *Indian Instant Pot Cookbook*. Combines two Apple Notes recipes.

## Part 1: Onion Masala

### Ingredients
- ¼ cup ghee or vegetable oil
- 2 cups diced onions
- 1 cup chopped tomatoes
- 8–10 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp minced ginger
- 1 jalapeño, minced (optional)
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp garam masala
- Salt to taste

### Method
1. On Instant Pot **Sauté (High)**, heat ghee or oil. Add jalapeño (if using), ginger, and garlic; cook about 2 minutes.
2. Add onions and sauté 7–8 minutes until golden.
3. Add tomatoes and cook 2 minutes. Stir in turmeric, cayenne, coriander, cumin, garam masala, and salt.
4. **Pressure-cooking note:** The original Apple Notes recipe lists no additional liquid and specifies **5 minutes on High**. Deglaze thoroughly. If needed, add a little water; **¼ cup is a suggested starting point, not an original-note measurement**.
5. Secure lid and pressure cook on High for **5 minutes** (Apple Notes method). Allow **10 minutes natural release**, then release remaining pressure.
6. If watery, simmer on Sauté for 3–4 minutes. Purée with an immersion blender.

**Timing note:** A separately published onion-masala method associated with Urvashi Pitre uses a longer pressure-cook time. This preserves the 5-minute timing from the original note pending a retest.

## Part 2: Chana Masala

### Ingredients
- 2 cups **cooked chickpeas** (canned or pre-cooked)
- 1 cup prepared onion masala
- 1 tbsp chana masala spice blend
- ½ tsp ground turmeric
- ½ tsp red chili powder, to taste
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp ghee or vegetable oil
- 1 cup water, adjusted for consistency
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro, for garnish

### Method
1. Heat ghee or oil in a pan over medium heat. Add chana masala spice blend, turmeric, and chili powder; cook about 30 seconds.
2. Add prepared onion masala and cook 2–3 minutes.
3. Stir in cooked chickpeas and water.
4. Bring to a gentle boil, reduce heat, and simmer 10 minutes.
5. Add garam masala, adjust salt, and simmer 2 more minutes.
6. Garnish with cilantro; serve with rice or naan.

## Notes
This combined recipe keeps the base masala and chickpea dish together. Chickpeas must be **cooked before** they go into this version of chana masala.
