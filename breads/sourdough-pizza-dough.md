# Sourdough Pizza Dough

**Status:** 🧪 Testing  
**Yield:** 3-4 pizzas  
**Category:** Breads

## Ingredients

- 500 g Tipo 00 flour
- 345 g water, 68-72°F
- 100 g active 100%-hydration sourdough starter
- 12 g fine sea salt

## Method

1. Combine the water and active starter.
2. Add the 00 flour and mix until no dry flour remains.
3. Rest briefly, then add the salt and mix until fully incorporated.
4. Bulk ferment at room temperature for about 1 1/2 hours, giving the dough 1-2 folds during bulk.
5. Divide the dough into equal portions and shape into tight dough balls.
6. Place the balls in covered containers and refrigerate for 12-24 hours.
7. Remove the dough balls from the refrigerator 2-3 hours before baking. Let them proof at room temperature until relaxed, extensible, and slightly puffy.
8. Working with one ball at a time, gently press outward from the center while preserving the rim. Stretch by hand to the desired size. Do not use a rolling pin.
9. Top lightly and bake immediately.

## Baking - Ooni

- Preheat the Ooni thoroughly.
- Target a stone temperature of about 750-850°F.
- Launch the pizza and begin turning once the crust has set.
- Rotate about every 15-20 seconds as needed for even browning.
- Bake until the crust is puffed, spotted, and cooked through, typically about 60-120 seconds.

## Notes

- Use 100% Tipo 00 flour. The earlier bread-flour substitution was a one-time pantry adjustment and is not part of the canonical formula.
- Ball the dough before the overnight cold proof.
- The final 2-3 hour room-temperature proof lets the dough relax before stretching.
- This recipe is intended for the Ooni.
- Keep toppings restrained for a Neapolitan-style bake.

## Results

- The fermentation formula is based on the previously tested sourdough pizza dough.
- Ooni baking is the current method and remains 🧪 Testing until the first Ooni bake is evaluated.
