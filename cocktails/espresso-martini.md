# Espresso Martini

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Last Tested:** 2026  
**Yield:** 1 cocktail  
**Category:** Cocktails

## Ingredients

- 2 oz Reyka vodka
- 1 oz fresh espresso
- 1 oz Mr Black Coffee Liqueur
- 1/2 oz simple syrup
- 3 coffee beans, for garnish

## Method

1. Add vodka, espresso, coffee liqueur, and simple syrup to a shaker with ice.
2. Shake very hard to create a foamy espresso head.
3. Fine-strain into a chilled coupe or martini glass.
4. Garnish with 3 coffee beans.

## Notes

- Reyka is the preferred Icelandic vodka.
