# Baked Mac & Cheese (Babish)

**Status:** 🧪 Testing — selected for Thanksgiving  
**Source:** [Babish — Mac and Cheese (Baked Mac & Cheese section)](https://www.babi.sh/recipes/macandcheese)

## Ingredients

### Béchamel / Mornay sauce
- 1½ lb cheese total, any combination of Parmesan, Gruyère, sharp white cheddar, Fontina, and low-moisture mozzarella
- ¼ cup butter
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- 4 cups cold whole milk
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Cayenne pepper, optional
- 2 tbsp whole-grain mustard

### Pasta and finishing cheese
- 1 lb dry pasta
- ½ lb cubed mozzarella
- 3 oz freshly grated cheddar, for topping
- 1 oz freshly grated Parmesan, for topping

## Method
1. Grate or cube cheeses. Reserve the separate mozzarella, cheddar, and Parmesan listed under *Pasta and finishing cheese*.
2. Cook pasta until just tender; drain.
3. Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium heat without browning. Whisk in flour and cook about 2–3 minutes.
4. Gradually whisk in cold milk, ensuring each addition is smooth. Bring to a bare simmer until slightly thicker than heavy cream.
5. Place the 1½ lb cheese blend in a heatproof bowl. Strain hot béchamel over the cheese and mix until melted and smooth.
6. Season with salt, pepper, optional cayenne, and mustard.
7. Preheat oven to **375°F**. Combine pasta and sauce in a casserole dish.
8. Fold in the separate ½ lb cubed mozzarella. Top with 3 oz cheddar and 1 oz Parmesan.
9. Bake about **45 minutes**, rotating halfway through, until golden and bubbling.
10. Rest **10 minutes** before serving.
