# Corinne's Kale Caesar Dressing

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star

## Ingredients
- 1 whole egg
- Juice of ½ lemon
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- 3 anchovy fillets, rinsed (optional, but recommended)
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- A dash of Worcestershire sauce
- A dash of Tabasco
- ½ cup olive oil
- Kale, for serving

## Method
1. Remove tough kale stems, chop leaves, and soak in **warm water for 10 minutes**. Drain thoroughly.
2. Optionally air-fry garlic cloves for about **5 minutes** to mellow them.
3. Blend all dressing ingredients **except oil** into a thick paste.
4. With blender running, slowly drizzle in oil until emulsified. An immersion blender works too.
5. Chill dressing for about 30 minutes. Thin with lemon juice or water if needed.
6. Toss with prepared kale.

## Food safety
Contains raw egg. Use pasteurized eggs when appropriate; keep chilled.
