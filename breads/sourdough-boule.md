# Sourdough Boule

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Last Tested:**  
**Yield:** 2 boules  
**Category:** Breads

## Ingredients

### Levain

- 35 g mature sourdough starter
- 35 g bread flour
- 35 g whole wheat flour
- 70 g water

### Dough

- 810 g bread flour
- 90 g whole wheat flour
- 680 g water
- 18 g fine sea salt
- All of the levain

## Method

This file preserves the boule-style ancestor of the Sourdough Pan Loaf. The original method includes autolyse, levain incorporation, strength-building folds, boule shaping, banneton proofing, and Dutch-oven baking.
