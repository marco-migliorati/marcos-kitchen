# Sausage and Baguette Stuffing

**Status:** ✅ Keeper  
**Category:** Thanksgiving

## Ingredients
- 1 baguette, cubed
- 1 lb sausage
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 celery stalks, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1½ sticks butter
- Sage, rosemary, and 1 bay leaf
- Warm stock, **quantity as needed** (not specified in original note)
- Salt and pepper

## Method
1. Dry baguette cubes in a **250°F oven** until firm and dry but not hard or cracking.
2. Brown sausage and remove, leaving fat in pan.
3. Sauté onion, celery, and garlic in the pan. Add butter, bay leaf, rosemary, and sage.
4. Combine vegetables and sausage. Toss with dried bread.
5. Gradually add warm stock until bread begins to soften without becoming soggy. Season, then rest 5 minutes.
6. Bake at **350°F for 30 minutes**, stirring halfway through for even browning.
