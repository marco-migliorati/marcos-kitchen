# Chocolate Cake with Ganache

**Status:** 🧪 Testing  
**Attribution:** Possibly Corinne's recipe; unconfirmed

## Cake ingredients
- 1½ cups sugar
- 1¼ cups flour
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp baking soda
- 1 cup milk
- 8 oz bittersweet chocolate, finely chopped
- ¾ cup cocoa powder
- 4 eggs
- ⅔ cup vegetable oil
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Ganache ingredients
- 1 lb milk chocolate, chopped
- ⅔ cup heavy cream
- 16 tbsp (2 sticks) butter

## Method
1. Mix sugar, flour, salt, and baking soda.
2. Warm milk, bittersweet chocolate, and cocoa over low heat, stirring until combined. Cool 5 minutes.
3. Add eggs, vanilla, and oil to the chocolate mixture; mix.
4. Stir in dry ingredients.
5. Lightly oil a 9 × 13-inch pan. Pour in batter.
6. Bake at **325°F for 30–35 minutes**, rotating halfway. Cool for about 2 hours.
7. For ganache, melt milk chocolate and cream in a double boiler, about 10 minutes.
8. Remove from heat and mix in butter. Chill 30–60 minutes.
9. Spread cooled ganache on the cooled cake.
