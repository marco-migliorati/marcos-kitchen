# Shorbat Adas

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** About 6 servings  
**Category:** Soups

## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, finely grated or minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1/4 tsp ground coriander
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 300 g red lentils, rinsed well
- 1.5 L (6 cups) vegetable or chicken stock
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- Juice of 1 lemon, plus more to taste

### For Serving

- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Lemon wedges
- Chopped parsley or cilantro, optional
- Warm pita, optional

## Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and carrots and cook until softened, about 7-10 minutes.
3. Add the garlic, cumin, turmeric, coriander, and black pepper. Cook for about 30-60 seconds, until fragrant.
4. Add the rinsed red lentils, stock, and 1 tsp kosher salt.
5. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer.
6. Cook uncovered for about 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils are completely soft and beginning to break down.
7. Blend with an immersion blender until smooth, or leave a little texture if preferred.
8. Stir in the lemon juice.
9. Taste and adjust the salt, lemon, and consistency. Add a little hot water or stock if the soup is thicker than desired.
10. Serve hot with a drizzle of olive oil and additional lemon.

## Notes

- Use red lentils. French green lentils were considered, but red lentils provide the soft, creamy texture characteristic of this soup.
- The lentils naturally thicken the soup; no dairy is necessary.
- Lemon is important to the finished flavor. Add it after cooking and adjust to taste.
- The soup will continue to thicken as it sits and may need additional water or stock when reheated.
