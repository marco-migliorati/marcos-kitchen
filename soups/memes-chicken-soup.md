# Meme's Chicken Soup

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Category:** Soups  
**Pasta:** Orzo preferred; Barilla Ditalini is also excellent

A comforting, tomato-based chicken soup passed down from Meme, Coco's French Canadian grandmother. Puréed vegetables give the broth body, while chicken and separately cooked pasta make it a satisfying meal.

## Ingredients

- 1 onion, roughly chopped
- 1 medium carrot, roughly chopped
- 1 rib celery, roughly chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided (2 tbsp for vegetables; 1 tbsp for chicken)
- 2 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 quart (946 ml) chicken broth
- 1 can (14 oz) whole tomatoes, preferably unseasoned; unseasoned tomato sauce also works
- Orzo or Barilla Ditalini, amount as desired
- Water, as needed to deglaze
- Up to 1 cup reserved pasta water, as needed
- Grated cheese, for serving

## Method

### 1. Sauté the vegetables

1. Heat **2 tbsp olive oil** in a Dutch oven over medium heat.
2. Add the onion, carrot, and celery. Cook for about **10 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the onion is translucent and the vegetables soften.
3. If the vegetables begin to stick, add a splash of water and scrape the bottom of the pot to deglaze.

### 2. Prepare the chicken

1. While the vegetables cook, remove the tenderloins from the chicken breasts.
2. Cube the breasts and tenderloins into bite-size pieces.

### 3. Add butter and garlic

1. Push the vegetables aside to make a well in the center of the pot.
2. Add **2 tbsp butter**. Once melted, add the garlic and cook for about **30 seconds**, until fragrant.
3. Stir the garlic and butter into the vegetables.

### 4. Purée the vegetables

1. Transfer the vegetable mixture to a tall, heat-safe container.
2. Carefully purée with an immersion blender until smooth. Set aside.

### 5. Brown the chicken

1. Return the Dutch oven to medium heat and add the remaining **1 tbsp olive oil**.
2. Add the cubed chicken and cook for about **2 minutes**, until it begins to brown. It does not need to be cooked through yet.
3. Return the vegetable purée to the pot and stir to combine.

### 6. Build the soup

1. Add **3 tbsp tomato paste** and stir until incorporated into the chicken and vegetables.
2. Pour in the chicken broth and stir.
3. If using whole canned tomatoes, purée them before adding. Add the puréed tomatoes or unseasoned tomato sauce and mix well.

### 7. Simmer

1. Bring the soup to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer.
2. Simmer as long as time allows to develop the flavors, stirring occasionally and adding liquid if it becomes too thick.
3. Make sure the chicken reaches **165°F** internally before serving.

### 8. Cook the pasta separately

1. While the soup simmers, cook the orzo or ditalini in a separate pot according to package directions.
2. Before draining, reserve **1 cup of pasta water**.
3. Drain the pasta. Add reserved pasta water to the soup a little at a time, if needed, to adjust the consistency.

### 9. Serve

1. Put cooked pasta into individual bowls.
2. Ladle the hot soup over the pasta.
3. Finish with grated cheese and enjoy.

**Storage tip:** Keep leftover soup and pasta separate so the noodles do not become mushy.

## Notes

- **Tomatoes:** Whole canned tomatoes are preferred, but purée them before adding. Plain, unseasoned tomato sauce is a good substitute.
- **Vegetable base:** Puréeing the sautéed vegetables creates a smooth, flavorful soup without cream or flour.
- **Pasta:** Orzo is the favorite, but Barilla Ditalini is also excellent. The quantity is flexible.
- **Consistency:** Add reserved pasta water gradually after the soup reduces, until it reaches the texture you like.
- **Simmering:** The longer, gentle simmer is part of the family method.
