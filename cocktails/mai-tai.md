# Mai Tai

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Last Tested:** 2026  
**Yield:** 1 cocktail  
**Category:** Cocktails

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 oz white rum
- 3/4 oz orange liqueur
- 3/4 oz fresh lime juice
- 1/2 oz orgeat
- 1/2 oz dark rum, for float

## Method

1. Add the white rum, orange liqueur, lime juice, and orgeat to a shaker with ice.
2. Shake well.
3. Strain over fresh ice.
4. Float the dark rum on top.

## Notes

- Lime was standardized at 3/4 oz to balance the sweetness of the orange liqueur and orgeat.
