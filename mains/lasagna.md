# Lasagna with Béchamel

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** One tray

## Sauce
- 1 lb ground meat
- 1 small carrot
- ½ celery rib
- 1 onion
- 5 garlic cloves
- 1 × 28 oz can peeled tomatoes
- 1 × 14 oz can tomato purée
- ½ cup red wine
- Olive oil, salt, pepper, oregano

## Pasta and cheese
- 2 eggs
- 180 g tipo 00 flour (90 g per egg), plus more if needed
- 1 lb **low-moisture block mozzarella, freshly shredded**
- 4 oz Pecorino Romano
- 4 oz Parmesan

## Béchamel
- **4 tbsp butter**
- **4 tbsp flour**
- **4 cups whole milk**

## Method
1. Brown ground meat and set aside. Sauté carrot, celery, onion, and garlic in olive oil until caramelized; blend vegetables to a paste.
2. Return meat, brown together, and deglaze with red wine. Add tomatoes, tomato purée, salt, pepper, and oregano. Simmer gently.
3. For pasta, incorporate eggs into flour; knead 10–15 minutes, wrap, and rest 30 minutes. Roll through the pasta machine, finishing around setting 6.
4. Briefly boil pasta sheets in salted water (the original note says about 1½ minutes), cool under running water, and drain.
5. For béchamel, melt butter, whisk in flour, then slowly add milk while whisking until smooth and thickened.
6. Spread a little sauce on the bottom of the baking dish. Layer pasta, tomato-meat sauce, béchamel, and cheeses.
7. **Alternate the orientation of pasta sheets** from lengthwise to crosswise in successive layers. This crosshatching strengthens the finished lasagna.
8. Finish with sauce, béchamel, and cheese. Cover and bake at **350°F for 15 minutes**; uncover and bake approximately **45 minutes** more.
9. Rest at least 10 minutes before slicing.

## House rules
- **No ricotta.**
- **Never use pre-shredded mozzarella.** Shred low-moisture block mozzarella the day of cooking.
- Béchamel replaces ricotta for the preferred texture.
