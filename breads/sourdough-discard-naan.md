# Sourdough Discard Naan

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** 12 naan

## Ingredients
- 110 g warm water
- 6 g instant yeast
- 150 g sourdough discard (100% hydration)
- 150 g plain Greek yogurt
- 35 g olive oil
- 12 g salt
- 500 g bread flour

Optional: melted butter, flaky salt, fresh herbs.

## Method
1. Mix the warm water and yeast. Add discard, yogurt, olive oil, and salt; stir.
2. Add flour and knead by hand for 10–12 minutes, until smooth and elastic. A mixer (8–10 minutes) also works, but hand mixing is preferred.
3. Place in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and rise until doubled, approximately 2 hours.
4. Divide into 12 balls (roughly 80–85 g each); rest covered for 15–20 minutes.
5. Roll into ovals 6–8 inches long and approximately ¼ inch thick.
6. Cook in a **dry** cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat for 1–2 minutes per side, until blistered and browned.
7. Serve plain, as preferred, or brush with butter and optional toppings.

## Notes
A practical use for sourdough discard. The toppings are entirely optional.
