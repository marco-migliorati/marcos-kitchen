# Empadão de Frango com Palmito

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Category:** Mains

## Mayonnaise crust
**Selected house standard (not yet retested):**
- **2½ cups all-purpose flour**
- **1¾ cups mayonnaise**
- Salt and pepper to taste

The original notes also contain an alternate ratio of 2 cups flour to 1½ cups mayonnaise. The two ratios are close; the larger, slightly firmer version is selected provisionally for its more detailed baking instructions. Adjust flour as needed for a cohesive, rollable dough.

### Crust method
1. Mix flour and mayonnaise into a cohesive dough. Chill for **30 minutes**.
2. Roll dough to line a savory pie dish; reserve dough for the top.
3. Add **cooled filling**, cover with remaining dough, and brush top with beaten egg.
4. Version B specifies **400°F for 10 minutes**, then **350°F for 30 minutes**, or until golden and cooked through.

## Chicken-and-palmito filling
- 2 chicken breasts, or chicken tenders for the preferred Instant Pot method
- ½ onion, chopped
- 6 garlic cloves, chopped
- ½ red bell pepper
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 8 oz cream cheese
- 1 can hearts of palm, drained and sliced
- 1 cup chopped olives
- Green onion and cilantro to taste
- Tomato sauce, as needed for a saucy filling (amount not specified in notes)

### Method
1. Cook chicken tenders in the Instant Pot, typically **2–3 minutes at high pressure** for thawed tenders, allowing for pressure buildup and checking that the chicken reaches **165°F (74°C)**. Shred.
2. Sauté onion, garlic, and bell pepper. Stir in tomato paste and enough tomato sauce to make a cohesive filling.
3. Add shredded chicken, hearts of palm, and olives; simmer until flavors combine and excess moisture reduces.
4. Stir cream cheese into the **simmering sauce** until smooth. Season with herbs and pepper.
5. Cool the filling before assembling the pie; bake using the mayonnaise-crust instructions.

## Vegetarian variation
Omit chicken and increase hearts of palm. The earlier vegetarian note used tomatoes, onion, garlic, olives, and a milk-and-Parmesan roux for creaminess.

## Family story
At Thanksgiving, cousin Ed returned for seconds, only to discover the empadão had vanished. After a family-wide search, Dad admitted he'd hidden it high in a cabinet behind other things so there would be leftovers. Despite the Thanksgiving memory, this is an everyday **Mains** recipe.
