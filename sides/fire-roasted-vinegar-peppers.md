# Fire-Roasted Vinegar Peppers

**Status:** ⭐ Gold Star  
**Yield:** About 1 pint  
**Category:** Sides & Salads

## Ingredients

- 4 large bell peppers, preferably red or a mix of red and yellow
- 120 g (1/2 cup) red wine vinegar
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- Pinch red pepper flakes, optional

## Method

1. Roast the whole peppers over a very hot grill, open flame, or other direct high heat, turning as needed, until the skins are deeply blackened and blistered on all sides.
2. Transfer the hot peppers to a covered bowl and let steam for about 15 minutes.
3. When cool enough to handle, peel away the loose blackened skin. Do not rinse the peppers under water, which washes away flavor.
4. Remove the stems and seeds.
5. Tear or slice the peppers into broad strips.
6. Combine the peppers with the red wine vinegar, sliced garlic, olive oil, kosher salt, oregano, and optional red pepper flakes.
7. Toss gently and refrigerate for at least several hours, preferably overnight, before serving.
8. Taste after marinating and adjust the salt and vinegar if needed.

## Serving

- Antipasto
- Sandwiches
- Sausage
- Grilled meats
- Crusty bread
- Cheese boards

## Notes

- Blacken the skins aggressively. The char is part of the flavor.
- Steam the peppers after roasting so the skins loosen easily.
- Do not rinse after peeling.
- Red wine vinegar provides the primary acidity; adjust after the peppers have had time to marinate.
- The garlic flavor strengthens as the peppers sit.
- Keep refrigerated.
